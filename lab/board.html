Sea glass,
set into a right angle.

Sea glass starts as broken glass. The sea turns the pieces over until every edge is soft, and someone picks them up one by one and sets them down in order. No two stones here are the same shape. Each mark is a handful of them, settled into a right angle.

Why a right triangleSquare and level, like a set square. It's also the corner of a field, which is the "field" in Fieldwise.
Why loose stonesThe work is many different things (site, search, tools, workflows), each with its own shape, arranged until they make one clear form.
The warm stoneOne citrine piece in almost every mark: the old favicon's amber dot, now the stone that's lit.
Built to printEvery mark also comes as flat, two-tone, one-colour and line versions, so it works on receipts and stamps as well as screens.

Nineteen arrangements

Every stone is generated as a broken shard tumbled smooth, then the stones are set into the corner largest first. The biggest pieces sit in the right angle and the small ones work out toward the tips. Each card also shows the other materials and the mark at 32 and 16 pixels. Press "Use this" to send one to the motion, lockups and print sections below.

Rearranging

On the site, the pieces start scattered and settle into the triangle, as if placed by hand. It plays once, then rests.

Pieces travel on one gentle spring and land in order from the right angle outward. With reduced motion turned on, it simply appears.

With the name

The wordmark is set in Google Sans Flex: plain, dark and quiet, so the glass does the talking. Inter Tight (the current site font) and a serif are there to compare.

In print

Print uses the flat, two-tone and one-colour versions. Glass is for screens and large covers. A receipt printer only has black, so the mark has to hold up as a solid shape.

Colours

CMYK values are starting points. Run a proof with your printer before a big order.

Getting the glass feel on paper

  • Print the two-tone mark on uncoated or soft-touch matte stock.
  • Put a spot UV or frosted varnish layer on the stones only. They catch the light and feel like glass under your thumb.
  • For a premium card, do the citrine stone in warm gold foil and keep the rest flat.
  • Blind-emboss the line version on the back of the card or the cover of a folder.

Smallest sizes

  • Loose and Settled (4–6 stones): 10 mm wide, or 24 px on screen.
  • Close-set and Nine stones: 14 mm, or 32 px.
  • Dense and Ring: 18 mm, or 40 px. Below that, switch to a Loose arrangement as the small version of the same mark.
  • Thermal receipts: the one-colour mark at 15–20 mm wide, never the line version.
Everything here is drawn in code as vectors, so any mark can be exported at any size as SVG, PDF or PNG once you've picked one.
